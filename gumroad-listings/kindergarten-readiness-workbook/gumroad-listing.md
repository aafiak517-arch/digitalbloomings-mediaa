## Description
**Help your child walk into kindergarten feeling confident.** 🎒✏️

This workbook covers the skills most kindergarten teachers hope to see on day one, in short, playful pages your child can finish in 10 to 15 minutes.

**What's inside (60 pages):**
- 🙋 All About Me + a parent "Ready for Kindergarten" skills checklist
- ✍️ I Can Write My Name practice page
- 🔤 26 letter pages, one for each letter A–Z. Kids color a big letter, learn a picture word (A is for apple…), trace uppercase and lowercase, write on their own, trace the word and find the letter
- 🔡 Match uppercase to lowercase (2 pages) + an alphabet chart
- 🔢 Numbers 1–20: color, count apples, trace and fill the ten-frames (10 pages)
- 🍎 How Many? counting (2 pages), What Number Is Missing?, Which Has More?, Dot to Dot 1–20
- 🔺 Trace the Shapes
- 👀 10 first sight words (I, a, see, the, can, like, we, my, is, and) + Read and Draw
- ✂️ 6 scissor-skills pages: straight, zigzag, wavy, curves, spiral and shapes
- ✅ Answer key + 🏆 "Ready for Kindergarten!" certificate

**Why parents love it:**
- Handwriting lines with a solid model letter first, then gray letters to trace
- Kid-friendly letter shapes (single-story "a" and "g", like in early readers)
- Original black-and-white pictures to color for every letter
- Ink-friendly for home printers
