**Build confident early readers, one word at a time.** 📚✏️

Sight words like *the, and, see, you* make up a big part of every early reader. Kids who know them by sight read faster and enjoy it more. This workbook takes your child through all 92 Dolch Pre-Primer and Primer words with short, fun daily practice.
